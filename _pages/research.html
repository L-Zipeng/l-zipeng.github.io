Scope and limitations

Levelized costs describe modeled production or delivery and omit market margins, tariffs, and price volatility. Results depend on financing, electricity mixes, technology learning, sustainable feedstock availability, and infrastructure. National averages can miss local conditions, and spatial estimates still require site-level validation. Prospective life-cycle impacts depend on the assumed background system and the plant design selected by the model.
